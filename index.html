Instant capture without the friction

Unfriction is a lightweight macOS overlay for dumping thoughts, snippets, tasks, or extracted text—instantly. Trigger it with a global shortcut in under 400ms, capture, and return to your flow. No windows. No setup. Local-only.

Key advantages

Global shortcut access

One keystroke opens the overlay anywhere on your Mac. Capture notes, clipboard items, or OCR text without leaving your app.

Smart tagging

Extracts and suggests tags as you type. Organize on the fly without extra effort.

Clipboard history & OCR

Access recent copies instantly. Extract text from screenshots or images using Apple's on-device OCR—offline, private.

Inline to-dos

Add checkboxes directly in notes. Simple lists that stay lightweight.

Local backups & export

Everything stored on your Mac. Automatic backups and one-click export. No accounts or sync required.

How it works

1. Hit the shortcut

Press your global hotkey (customizable). Overlay appears in 400ms.

2. Capture instantly

Type a note, paste from clipboard history, or drop an image for OCR. Add tags or checkboxes as needed.

3. Dismiss and done

Hit Escape or Enter. Note saves locally. Return to your work.

4. Access later

Reopen anytime. Search, export, or backup with one click.
